Privacy policy

claudegmail · Last updated November 6, 2026

claudegmail is a personal tool that runs entirely on its owner's computer. It connects the owner's Gmail accounts to Claude. This policy explains what it accesses and what happens to that data.

What it accesses

With your permission, claudegmail uses the Gmail API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify. This lets it:

  • search and read your email messages, threads and labels,
  • create drafts and send email when you ask it to,
  • mark messages read or unread, archive, star, label, and move messages to Trash.

It can't permanently delete email.

Where your data goes

  • Sign-in tokens are saved only on your own computer, in a file that only your user account can read (~/.gmail-multi-mcp/accounts.json). They aren't uploaded anywhere.
  • Email content is fetched from Google's Gmail API directly to your computer, only when you ask Claude to do something with your mail. claudegmail passes the result to the Claude app you're using. Anthropic then handles it under the privacy terms of your Claude account.
  • claudegmail has no servers of its own. It doesn't collect, store, sell or share your data with anyone, and it doesn't use your data for advertising or to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

claudegmail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect an account at any time by running node src/auth-cli.js remove <account>, which deletes its token from your computer. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
